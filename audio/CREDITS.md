# Audio credits — 猫猫小岛

All files were trimmed, high-passed at 90 Hz, faded, loudness-matched (about -17 dBFS active RMS, peak at -1 dBFS or lower) and re-encoded to mono 44.1 kHz MP3 at 96 kbps. The BGM tracks are stereo MP3 at 96 kbps. Every source is CC0 or public domain, so **no attribution is legally required**; the credits below are a courtesy.
